WHO WE ARE

Chloe and Noah are a Philadelphia-based acoustic duo performing at weddings, private events, corporate functions, and Atlantic City casinos, known for a polished yet relaxed sound and engaging atmosphere.

With Chloe’s training at Susquehanna University and Noah’s lifelong guitar playing, their performances strike a natural balance between polish and personality, delivering warm, groove-driven sets that feel both intimate and engaging.
